# Code of Conduct

Author: Fabrizio Musacchio  
Date: June 2026

ZenReg follows a simple rule: be constructive, respectful, and precise.

Contributors and users are expected to:

- communicate with care and professional courtesy;
- assume good intent while still being clear about technical concerns;
- keep discussions focused on improving the software and its scientific use;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or exclusionary behavior.

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be sent to the project maintainer.
